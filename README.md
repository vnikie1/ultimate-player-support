# Ultimate Player — Support & Privacy

Support and legal pages for the **Ultimate Player** Android app.

- **Privacy Policy:** [`index.html`](index.html) — hosted via GitHub Pages for the Google Play listing.
- **Contact:** vnikie1@gmail.com

Ultimate Player is a media player for content you already own — network shares (SMB/WebDAV),
your own Plex/Jellyfin servers, DLNA/UPnP, local folders, downloads, and direct URLs. It has no
accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking; your data stays on your device.
